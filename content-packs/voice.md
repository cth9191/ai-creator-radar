# Chase AI voice guide

This file is sent to Claude as the style guide for every content pack. Edit it
freely: the next refresh picks up the change and regenerates the packs.

Channel: Chase AI (YouTube @Chase-H-AI). Audience: builders, solo creators and
small-business people who want to actually use new AI tools, not just hear
about them.

How Chase sounds:
- Direct and practical. Leads with what the thing does for you and whether it
  is worth your time.
- Hands-on: "I tested it", "here's how to set it up", "here's where it broke".
  Shows the work rather than speculating.
- Plain words, short sentences, a little energy. Confident without hype.
- Honest about limits and trade-offs. Calls out when something is overhyped.
- Speaks to the viewer as "you". No corporate tone, no filler intros like
  "In today's video".

Things to avoid:
- Clickbait that the video can't pay off, fake urgency, "game changer",
  "insane", "mind-blowing", "this changes everything".
- Claims the story doesn't support. If a detail is unclear, frame it as
  something the video will test or check.
- Emoji walls and hashtag stuffing. At most one or two hashtags on X.
